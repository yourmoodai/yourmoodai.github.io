# Company privacy policy

Your Mood, Inc. operates `yourmood.ai` and the informational website at
`savior.yourmood.ai`. This policy covers website visits, communications to the
company, and the optional acceptance-receipt service. The
[Savior privacy notice](/legal/savior/privacy/) describes local software processing.

## Collection and choice

We limit collection to information needed for a stated purpose. Optional
analytics, telemetry, diagnostic uploads, and persisted local activity records
require a separate affirmative choice. Declining them does not prevent core
Savior rewriting. A choice to retain local history does not authorize uploads.
Agreeing to terms does not authorize analytics or model training.

Before an optional activity begins, we explain its purpose, information,
recipients, retention, and withdrawal. Withdrawal stops future optional
collection and transmission, including queued optional uploads. Request deletion
of existing managed records separately. A new optional purpose requires a new
choice; updates do not silently expand an earlier one.

We do not operate remote product analytics. If introduced, they will require
separate opt-in and contain only minimal, content-free aggregate usage or
reliability measurements. Prompts, outputs, files, credentials, persistent
cross-service identifiers, and centrally retained individual event histories
are excluded. Aggregation alone does not establish anonymity, especially for
small groups or repeated measurements.

## Websites and providers

The website source contains no advertising or browser analytics scripts.
GitHub Pages hosts our public pages and records visitor IP addresses for
security under [GitHub's practices](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).
Following an external link creates a separate interaction with that service.
Local inference does not make website visits or downloads anonymous.

Our email provider processes communications you send to us. The optional receipt
service, when enabled, uses Amazon Web Services in `us-east-1` for verification
email, private storage, request processing, and signing. These providers handle
operational and network information under their own applicable agreements.
Company access restrictions do not prevent every provider or infrastructure
administrator from inspecting information.

## Support and public contributions

We use your email address, message, and deliberately supplied attachments to
respond to and resolve your request. Please remove secrets and unnecessary
personal information. We do not silently attach prompts or files to support
reports. A support submission is not analytics or training consent.

Company-controlled support messages are deleted 90 days after the issue closes,
subject to necessary legal retention. Provider backups follow their own deletion
cycles; we do not promise immediate erasure from them. Local Savior deletion
does not remove an email sent to the company.

Public GitHub issues and contributions can be read, indexed, or copied by others.
Use email for private requests; do not post credentials or identity documents.

## Acceptance receipts

The receipt service is optional and is not required to exercise AGPL software
rights. It verifies control of an email address, presents an exact agreement
version, and records an explicit acceptance action. It retains that address,
document version and digest, action, server timestamps, and a receipt signature.
It does not verify legal identity or collect prompt content.

Receipts remain private while the separate receipt-service agreement is active
and for three years after closure, subject to applicable legal requirements.
Verification challenges expire after 15 minutes; expired challenges cannot
authorize acceptance. Temporary uploads and expired challenge state are cleaned
up separately. Minimal security records are restricted to abuse prevention and
operations, with a seven-day application-log retention period. Provider security
records are distinct from optional product analytics.

## Access, protection, and deletion

We restrict staff and service roles to information needed for a documented
purpose and review privileged access. Stored receipt data is encrypted at rest
by AWS and transferred over HTTPS. Receipt signatures establish integrity; they
do not make the contents end-to-end encrypted or unreadable to administrators.
Logs must exclude receipt bodies, email addresses, verification tokens, and
presigned URLs. We do not sell receipt information or use it for advertising,
product analytics, or model training.

You can retrieve your receipts and close the service using email verification.
Contact [support@yourmood.ai](mailto:support@yourmood.ai) for access, correction,
deletion, or other applicable privacy requests. Verification is proportionate to
the request. We explain any records retained for legal reasons and applicable
complaint or appeal routes. We cannot retrieve device-only information never
sent to us or remove copies others control.

## Changes

The version and effective date displayed with this policy identify the applicable
text. Approved versions and publication receipts are linked from the legal
index. A change in wording does not authorize a new data use or replace required
consent. A publication receipt demonstrates observed publication, not compliance
with every law or acceptance by every visitor.
