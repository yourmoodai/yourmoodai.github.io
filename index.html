Savior

A closer look at what you send.

Savior is an open source mod for Claude Code. When it detects an offensive phrase in a supported prompt, you can choose its replacement and inspect exactly what changed. The surrounding text stays intact.

After setup, detection runs offline on your computer. Full-text recording is off by default. Savior is currently in development.
